Latent Space

A three-stage generative pipeline: an extracted color palette seeds an anchor character image, which is then traversed across its latent neighborhood to produce a family of consistent variants.

01

Color palette

Adobe Color

The monochromatic gold-olive scheme that anchors the entire piece — one hue walked across the value range, generated and refined in Adobe's color tooling.

02

Anchor image

Midjourney · Niji 7

A single full-resolution character sheet generated from the palette — the fixed reference point every variant is measured against. Built for clarity: even lighting, flat background, strong silhouette.

03

Latent space variants

Higgsfield.ai

Eight variants derived from the anchor — pose, angle, and expression traversals across its latent neighborhood while identity and palette hold steady.

04

Motion

Kling 3.0

The character brought into motion — a short generated sequence that extends the still latent variants into time.
