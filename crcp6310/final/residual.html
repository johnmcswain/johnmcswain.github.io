Why two renderersPrediction is continuous math, so it draws as SVG. Measurement is quantized and physical, so it draws as a dithered pixel buffer at quarter resolution.
The particlesSpawn rate follows the residual. Flat water throws almost nothing. A surge tears spray off the surface.
The moonPhase for this window sets the key light. Near full, the prediction line brightens and the caustics run hot. Near new, everything dims.
HapticsOn an Xbox controller the residual under the playhead drives the motors. Surge goes to the heavy one, so you feel the storm arrive.
