How it works. Each system is integrated with RK4 at a fixed time step, so moving the polygons at a constant rate makes them naturally speed up where the flow is fast and slow where it is slow — the motion's character comes from the attractor itself. Polygons are drawn with beginShape / vertex / endShape; fractal depth runs a recursive Koch step on each one. The skiatron (light) theme renders everything as a monochrome dark trace; colour palettes apply in the dark theme.
