02

The Problem

Most AI Systems Run Unseen by the People Who Operate Them

Cost, speed, reliability, and safety pull against each other. The consequences of a design choice arrive late, in another team's queue, tangled with other causes.

Teams learn these trade-offs at two in the morning, in production, after the budget is spent. A simulator gives them a place to learn earlier.

03

Flagship Simulator

Decision Studio

Move across the system. Untraced components stay in fog. Launch Decision Studio

Design an AI workflow under a budget. Then run it for a shift while a model, acting as game master, studies your design and breaks it at its weakest point.

Injection, outages, runaway spend, failed hardware, and misplaced data. Trace a component and you see the cause. Leave it dark and you see only the effect on everything downstream.
